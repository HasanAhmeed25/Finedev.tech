## assets/images


Place images with these filenames in the assets/images folder. If you do not have images, use free illustrations from sites such as undraw.co or use placeholders.


- `hero-illustration.png` — 1200×800 or SVG illustration
- `service-webdev.png` — 800×600 (screenshot/graphic)
- `service-appdev.png` — 800×600
- `favicon.ico` — 32×32 icon
